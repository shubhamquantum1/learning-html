<h2> 📁 Mini-Project 2: Linked Multi-Page Portfolio </h2>

For this project, you will build a multi-page website consisting of <b>three interconnected pages</b> inside your `projects` folder.

You will write the code for these pages yourself, and I will act as your QA validator.

<b>The Pages:</b><br>
1. <b>`portfolio.html`</b> (The homepage of your portfolio)
2. <b>`about.html`</b> (A page all about you)
3. <b>`hobbies.html`</b> (A page showing off your hobbies with images)

<b>Requirements for EVERY Page:</b><br>
* Must use the full standard HTML5 boilerplate.<br>
* Must have a proper semantic structure containing `<header>, <nav>, <main>, and <footer>` tags.<br>
* The `<nav>` section on <b>every</b> page must contain links to all three pages so you can click around from anywhere.<br>
    * Tip: Since all three files will sit in the same `projects/` folder, they can link to each other directly by filename (e.g., `<a href="about.html">About Me</a>`).<br>
* The `<footer>` must contain a copyright notice with the &copy; symbol.<br>

<b>Page-Specific Requirements:</b><br>
1. <b>portfolio.html:</b>
    * An `<h1>` header welcoming visitors.
    * A short intro paragraph (`<p>`) with at least one bold (`<strong>`) and one italicized (`<em>`) word.
    * A structural `<div>` containing a list (`<ul>` or `<ol>`) of 3 skills you have or want to learn.
2. <b>about.html:</b>
    * An `<h1>` header like "About [Your Name]".
    * An `<h2>` sub-heading.
    * At least 2 paragraphs of text describing your journey or background (use a `<br>` somewhere).
3. <b>hobbies.html:</b>
    * An `<h1>` header.
    * A list of at least 3 hobbies.
    * At least two images showing your hobbies.
        * Tip: You can use free web placeholder images like `https://picsum.photos/400/300` for the `src` attribute (e.g., `<img src="https://picsum.photos/400/300" alt="Random pretty picture" width="300">`). Or, if you want me to generate custom images for you (like coding, gaming, reading, etc.), just ask!
